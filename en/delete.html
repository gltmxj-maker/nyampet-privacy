Account and data deletion request

How to delete your account and data in the food-log pet app Nyampet (Android). You can request deletion even if you have already removed the app or never installed it.

If you only used the app as a guest

Records made without signing in are stored only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app. Photos sent for analysis and purchase records may exist on our servers, so you can request deletion with method 2 below. If you bought something as a guest, include your Google Play order number (starting with GPA.) to verify it is you. If you only sent photos without buying anything — photos sent for analysis are not stored on our servers (they are processed only during analysis), so there is nothing to request. Uninstalling the app removes all records on your device.

Method 1 — In the app

If you still use the app, this is the fastest way. Your request is accepted right away; you can cancel it in the app for 30 days, and after that period it is deleted within one day.

Settings → Delete account and data

You confirm twice, and before that you can export your records to a file.

Method 2 — By email

If you have already removed the app, send a request to the address below.

Request deletion at contact@cinevyze.com

Nyampet does not store the email address of your login account, so we do not verify you by email. If possible, reinstall the app, sign in with the same account and delete it with method 1. If that is not possible, include the sign-in method you used (Google or Kakao) and roughly when you signed up, and, if you made a purchase, your Google Play order number (starting with GPA.). We will process the request after checking.

Deadline

We delete your data within 30 days of receiving your request. Most requests are completed within a few business days, and we reply when done.

What is deleted and what is kept

When deletion is carried out (after the 30-day grace period for requests made in the app), your account and usage records are all deleted. However, payment records that the law requires us to keep cannot be deleted right away; they are converted into a form that cannot identify you and kept only for the legal period.

ItemWhat happens

Payment and purchase records Kept for 5 years after pseudonymization
The original receipt token is destroyed immediately and the link to your account is removed. Only the product name, payment platform and time of payment remain, in a form that cannot identify you, and they are deleted completely and automatically after 5 years.

Why only payment records are kept

Korea's Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce requires records of payment and supply to be kept for 5 years, and tax law requires the same for books. This lets late refunds or payment disputes be checked. These records alone cannot show whose payment it was.

Remaining credits

When you delete your account, your remaining credits and purchased outfit items are also removed and cannot be restored. Refunds follow the Google Play refund policy.

Contact

If you have questions about the progress or scope of deletion, contact us any time at contact@cinevyze.com. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
