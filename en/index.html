Privacy Policy

Applies to the Android app Nyampet · Effective October 27, 2026 · The Korean version is the governing text.

Nyampet is a food-log app where your pet grows as you record what you eat. We collect only the information needed to provide the service, and nothing else. You can start as a guest without signing up; in that case your records stay on your device.

Sensitive information (health information)

Meal records, meal photos and body records may count as health information, so we ask for separate consent the first time a photo is analyzed. If you do not consent, you can still log meals by typing their names.

What we do not collect

  • We do not access your location, contacts or call logs.
  • We do not currently use any crash-reporting or usage-analytics tools. If we introduce one, we will update and announce this policy first.
  • We do not show personalized (behavior-based) ads; ads appear only in the shop when you choose to watch one.
  • Selfies with your pet and pet-album pictures are saved only to your device's photo album and never sent to our servers.
  • App lock (fingerprint or face) uses only your device's own authentication; we never receive biometric data.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your device only. We do not create or collect device tokens for server push.

2. Retention and deletion

When you request account deletion, you can cancel the request in the app for 30 days; after that, the information above is deleted within one day. Photos sent for analysis are not stored on Nyampet's servers (they are processed only during analysis). Photos sent to the analysis provider (Google) are processed only within the outsourcing terms below. Nyampet does not use these photos for model training, and the provider's retention is limited to what the Google Cloud data processing terms allow. One exception applies because the law requires it:

Payment and purchase records — kept for 5 years after pseudonymization Korea's Act on Consumer Protection in Electronic Commerce requires payment and supply records to be kept for 5 years, and tax law requires the same for books. When your account is deleted, the original receipt token is destroyed immediately and the link to your account is removed; only the product, payment platform and time of payment remain, in a form that cannot identify you. They are deleted completely after 5 years.

Deletion method — electronic files are deleted so that they cannot be recovered.

Information collected directly by the ad SDK

When you choose to watch a rewarded ad in the shop, the ad SDK Google AdMob directly collects your advertising ID and ad-interaction information to show ads and prevent invalid clicks. Nyampet requests ads only with personalized ads turned off, and only after asking for any consent required in your region. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.

We do not sell personal information to any other third party or provide it for advertising purposes.

5. Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct, delete or stop the processing of your personal information, and you can withdraw consent to sensitive-information processing and measurement at any time in the app settings. In the app, go to Settings → Delete account and data; if you have already removed the app, contact us by email.

How to delete your account and data

6. Security measures

  • Per-account access control keeps each user's data separated from other users.
  • Data in transit is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Data on your device is excluded from device backups.
  • Original payment receipt tokens are not stored as-is.

7. Children under 14

Children under 14 may not use Nyampet. We check this with the birth year entered at first launch, and we do not knowingly collect children's personal information. If we learn that we have, we delete it immediately.
